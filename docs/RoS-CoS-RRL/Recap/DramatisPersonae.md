# Dramatis Personae, Places, and Campaign Reference

> GM reference: contains character secrets and adventure spoilers. Relationships checked against DragnaCarta's Curse of Strahd: Reloaded are distinguished from party recollections. Campaign circumstances may change as play continues.

Combined from DramatisPersonae.md, places-and-persons-of-interest.md, npcs.md, and locations.md. Erasmus is Erasmus van Richten, as confirmed by the campaign owner. Original journal URLs and case-sensitive identifiers are preserved; missing closing Markdown parentheses are repaired. Linked journal contents and live link validity have not been checked.

## Durst Manor and the Countryside

### The Dursts and Klara
Elisabeth and Gustav Durst were wealthy Barovian merchants who owned a windmill. The earlier notes list Rosavalda (Rose), Thornboldt (Thorn), and Walter with the family.

Gustav had an affair with the family's nursemaid, Klara, who bore Walter. Walter was Klara and Gustav's child, not Elisabeth's biological son. Elisabeth murdered Gustav, tortured Klara, and bound Walter's soul to his corpse; he subsequently grew into an undead flesh creature. The campaign notes also record Elisabeth starving Rose and Thorn to death while leading a cannibalistic cult in the mansion.

The manuscript spells her name Elisabeth; the earlier notes used Elizabeth.

### Dr. Rudolph van Richten
A famous monster hunter who aided the revolt against Strahd. The party's notes record his whereabouts as unknown. Arturi is looking for him.

He is Erasmus van Richten's father and Ezmerelda d'Avenir's mentor. His deceased son is now an Ethereal spirit whom Rudolph initially cannot perceive or communicate with.

## Village of Barovia

- Ismark Kolyanovich: current Burgomaster of Barovia. Known as “the Lesser,” a slight comparing him with his ancestor Ismark the Great.
- Ireena Kolyana: adopted daughter of the late Burgomaster Kolyan Indirovich and adopted sister of Ismark. She has no memories of her earlier life. The campaign notes say she was found at Castle Ravenloft's gates; the manuscript's historical account instead places her discovery near the village of Barovia. These accounts are retained as a campaign/source difference.
- Bildrath Cantemir: named in the records; no further description supplied.
- Parriwimple: a hulking but simple man the party met outside the Barovian church.
- Father Donavich: priest of the Church of Barovia and Doru's father.
- Doru: Donavich's son and Gertruda's betrothed. Led a failed revolt against Strahd and was turned into a vampire spawn.
- Gertruda: Mary's daughter and Doru's betrothed. The party notes record her as missing, presumed to have gone to the castle. Reloaded describes her late father as a merchant and Mary as her overbearing, widowed mother.
- Mary: Gertruda's mother.

### GM-only: Ireena's Birth Family
Ireena is Izek Strazni's biological sister, a relationship she initially does not know. Her public name is Ireena Kolyana; the older campaign notes called her Ireena Kolyanovich.

## Tser Pool Encampment and the Vistani

- Madam Eva: a fortune teller who visited the party in a dream and asked them to see her at Tser Pool.

## Town of Vallaki

### Blue Water Inn
- Urwin Martikov and Danika Dorakova: husband and wife who co-own the inn. Urwin is the father of Brom and Bray Martikov.
- Brom and Bray Martikov: listed at the inn in the NPC index.
- Rictavio: a flamboyant half-elf carnival ringmaster with a knack for storytelling; seems to be hiding something.
- Muriel Vinshaw: listed under the inn in the dramatis personae. A Keepers of the Feather agent and the former fiancée of Davian's deceased son Elric Martikov.
- Szoldar Szoldarovich and Yevgeni Krushkin: listed under the inn heading in the NPC index; no further details supplied.

### St. Andral's Church
- Father Lucian Petrovich: the priest.
- Yeska: a boy in Father Lucian's care.
- Milivoj: the church's groundskeeper.
- Henrik van der Voort: Vallaki's coffin maker; the party met him outside the church.

### Vallakovich Manor
- Baron Vargas Vallakovich: a maniacal ruler who believes happiness will save him and his town. He punishes “malicious unhappiness” severely and holds events to raise morale. At the time of the notes, the previous event was the Wolf's Head Jamboree and the next was the Festival of the Blazing Sun.
- Izek Strazni: the Baron's cruel henchman, with a deformed, monstrous arm. Has been staring at Ireena. Earlier notes spelled his name Isek. See the GM-only birth-family note above.
- Victor Vallakovich: named in the dramatis personae and NPC index.

### Wachterhaus
- Lady Fiona Wachter: openly hostile towards the Baron. The notes say he will not move against her because she is distantly related to Strahd.
- Nikolai and Karl Wachter: Fiona's sons; openly hostile towards the Baron. Also listed under the Blue Water Inn heading in the NPC index.
- Stella Wachter: named in the dramatis personae and NPC index.
- Erasmus van Richten: the “Erasmus” listed here in the original notes, as confirmed by the campaign owner. He is Rudolph van Richten's deceased son, not a member of the Wachter family. His appearance is associated with The Lost Soul and Victor Vallakovich's story. Listing him here records a campaign association, not a family relationship.

### Blinsky Toys
- Gadof Blinsky: a strange toy maker who makes macabre toys. Madam Eva told the party to buy a toy from him for Arabelle. He made a doll that looks exactly like Ireena and was very nervous about it.

### Vistani Encampment near Vallaki
- Luvash: Arabelle's father and Arrigal's elder brother. Co-leader of the camp with Arrigal.
- Arrigal: Luvash's younger brother and Arabelle's paternal uncle. Co-leader of the camp.
- Kasimir Velikov: named at the encampment; no further description supplied in the original notes.

## Wizard of Wines and the Martikov Family

Davian Martikov is the family's patriarch. The checked relationships are:

- Davian is Urwin's father and the father of the deceased Elric.
- Urwin is Danika Dorakova's husband, Brom and Bray's father, and a sibling of Adrian, Elvir, and Stefania Martikov.
- Stefania is married to Dag Tomescu, who married into the Martikov family.
- Claudiu is explicitly identified as Stefania's son.
- Elric was engaged to Muriel Vinshaw, who continues to grieve him.
- The winery scene identifies Martin and Viggo as two boys and Yolanda as the infant accompanying the family. Their exact parentage was not established in the passages retrieved during the relationship check and is not asserted here.

### Unresolved Source Inconsistency
Urwin's profile calls him Stefania's older brother, while the “Meet the Martikovs” material says Stefania is five years older than Urwin. They are recorded simply as siblings here. One appendix passage spells her name Stefnia; this reference uses Stefania, as in the narrative.

## Orders and Groups

### Vistani
Nomadic people who live in caravans. The notes say they saved Strahd when he was mortally wounded hundreds of years ago, while he was alive. In gratitude, he promised them safe passage through his lands. That agreement still holds: they can come and go through the mists.

Many Barovians distrust the Vistani because they believe them to be Strahd's spies.

### Order of the Silver Dragon
See the Argynvostholt section above.
