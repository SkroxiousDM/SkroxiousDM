# Dramatis Personae, Places, and Campaign Reference

> GM reference: contains character secrets and adventure spoilers. Campaign circumstances may change as play continues.

Characters are grouped by their first identifiable, direct encounter with the party. Dreams, rumours, second-hand mentions, and unidentified appearances do not count. Identifiable spirits do count. Remains-only encounters and people never met are labelled separately. Locations follow the campaign owner's confirmations and the party journal, not the adventure's default encounter sequence.

All 70 original NPC journal destinations and all 19 enabled location destinations are retained. URL spelling and case-sensitive identifiers are unchanged; live link validity has not been checked. Subsections use the location journal of their enclosing area where no dedicated location link was supplied.

## Durst Manor

No dedicated location-journal link was supplied for Durst Manor.

### The Dursts and Klara
Elisabeth and Gustav Durst were wealthy Barovian merchants who owned a windmill. Rosavalda (Rose), Thornboldt (Thorn), and Walter are listed with the family in the original notes.

Gustav had an affair with the family's nursemaid, Klara, who bore Walter. Walter was Klara and Gustav's child, not Elisabeth's biological son. Elisabeth murdered Gustav, tortured Klara, and bound Walter's soul to his corpse; he subsequently grew into an undead flesh creature. The campaign notes also record Elisabeth starving Rose and Thorn to death while leading a cannibalistic cult in the mansion.

The party encountered Klara's ghost in a mirror, Rose and Thorn's ghosts in the attic, Gustav's animated flayed skin in the crypts, Walter within the flesh creature, and Elisabeth's ghost during their escape. The manuscript spells her name Elisabeth; the earlier notes used Elizabeth.

## Orders and Groups

### Vistani
Nomadic people who live in caravans. The notes say they saved Strahd when he was mortally wounded hundreds of years ago, while he was alive. In gratitude, he promised them safe passage through his lands. That agreement still holds: they can come and go through the mists.

Many Barovians distrust the Vistani because they believe them to be Strahd's spies.

See the Argynvostholt section above.

## Editorial and Source Notes

- Encounter placements follow the campaign owner's confirmations and the party journal. First meetings exclude dreams and rumours; identifiable spirit encounters count. Merely learning a character's home or family does not establish an encounter.
- Erasmus is Erasmus van Richten, as confirmed by the campaign owner.
- Morgantha and her daughters have separate first encounters despite sharing a journal destination. Arabelle was first met in Rictavio's wagon at Arasek Stockyard.
