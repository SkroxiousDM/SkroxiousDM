Links retain the original URLs and case-sensitive journal IDs; missing closing Markdown parentheses have been repaired. Link validity and linked-page contents have not been checked.

## Durst Manor and the Countryside

### The Dursts

Elizabeth, Gustav, Rosavalda (Rose), Thornboldt (Thorn), and Walter were wealthy Barovian merchants who owned a windmill. Elizabeth went mad during Gustav’s affair with the nursemaid Klara. She killed Gustav, tortured Klara, turned their son into a flesh monster, and starved Rose and Thorn to death while leading a cannibalistic cult in the mansion.

### Dr. Rudolph van Richten

Also recorded as Rudolph Van Richten and Van Richten. A famous monster hunter who aided the revolt against Strahd. His current whereabouts are unknown. Arturi is looking for him. The NPC index adds the title ‘Dr.’.

[NPC journal — Dr. Rudolph van Richten](https://skroxiousdm.github.io/SkroxiousDM/simple-quest/lore/journalentry.cgswpn6ymjxd4y5s/journalentry.cgswpn6ymjxd4y5s.journalentrypage.hgasguu4yoewak8i)

## Orders and Groups

### Vistani
Nomadic people who live in caravans. According to the notes, they saved Strahd when he was mortally wounded hundreds of years ago, while he was still alive. He promised them safe passage through his lands in gratitude. This agreement still holds: the Vistani can come and go through the mists.

Many Barovians distrust the Vistani because they believe them to be Strahd’s spies.
