# Places and Persons of Interest


## Durst Manor and the Countryside

### The Dursts
Elizabeth, Gustav, Rosavalda (Rose), Thornboldt (Thorn), and Walter were wealthy Barovian merchants who owned a windmill.

Elizabeth went mad during Gustav's affair with the nursemaid Klara. She killed Gustav, tortured Klara, turned their son into a flesh monster, and starved Rose and Thorn to death while leading a cannibalistic cult in the mansion.

### Rudolph Van Richten
A famous monster hunter who aided the revolt against Strahd. His current whereabouts are unknown. Arturi is looking for him.

## Village of Barovia

### Ismark Kolyanovich
The current Burgomaster of Barovia. Known as “the Lesser,” a slight comparing him unfavourably with his ancestor Ismark the Great.

### Ireena Kolyanovich
Ismark's adopted sister. The Kolyanovich family found her at the gates of Castle Ravenloft when she was very young. She remembers nothing from before that time.

### Bildrath Cantemir
Named in the dramatis personae; no further details recorded.

### Parriwimple
A hulking but simple man the party met outside the Barovian church.

### Father Donavich
Priest of the Church of Barovia and Doru's father.

### Doru
Father Donavich's son. He led a failed revolt against Strahd and was turned into a vampire. Gertruda is his betrothed.

### Gertruda
Doru's betrothed and Mary's daughter. Currently missing; presumed to have gone to the castle.

### Mary
Gertruda's mother.

## Town of Vallaki

### Morgantha
A peddler selling addictive dream pastries to Barovian refugees.

### Blue Water Inn

- Urwin Martikov and Danika Dorakova: husband and wife who own the inn.
- Rictavio: a flamboyant half-elf carnival ringmaster with a knack for storytelling. Seems to be hiding something.
- Muriel Vinshaw: named under the inn in the dramatis personae; no further details recorded.

### St. Andral's Church

- Father Lucian Petrovich: the church's priest.
- Yeska: a boy in Father Lucian's care.
- Milivoj: the church's groundskeeper.
- Henrik van der Voort: Vallaki's coffin maker, whom the party encountered outside the church.

### Vallakovich Manor

- Baron Vargas Vallakovich: a maniacal ruler who believes happiness will save him and his town. He punishes “malicious unhappiness” severely and holds events to raise morale. At the time of the notes, the previous event was the Wolf's Head Jamboree and the next was the Festival of the Blazing Sun.
- Izek Strazni: the Baron's cruel henchman, with a deformed, monstrous arm. Has been staring at Ireena. 
- Victor Vallakovich: Son of Baron Vargas Vallakovich

### Wachterhaus

- Lady Fiona Wachter: openly hostile towards the Baron. The notes say he will not move against her because she is distantly related to Strahd.
- Nikolai and Karl Wachter: Fiona's sons; openly hostile towards the Baron.
- Stella Wachter: daughter of Lady Fiona Wachter:
- Erasmus: listed under Wachterhaus in the dramatis personae; no further details recorded.

### Blinsky Toys

- Gadof Blinsky: a strange toy maker who creates macabre toys. Madam Eva told the party to buy a toy from him for Arrabell / Arabell. He made a doll that looks exactly like Ireena and was very nervous about it.

## Argynvostholt 
Headquarters of the Order of the Silver Dragon
A revenant the party met at Tser Falls advised them to travel west to Argynvostholt, where Sir Godfrey Gwilym awaits those who would raise their swords against Strahd. The Order opposed Strahd in life.

- Sir Godfrey Gwilym: the party has been told to seek him out.
- Commander Vladimir Horngaard: also called “Sir Vladimir Horngaard” in the notes. A commander of the Order who gave an order not to attack Strahd.
- Brother Marek: named in the dramatis personae; no further details recorded.

## Orders and Groups

### Vistani
Nomadic people who live in caravans. According to the notes, they saved Strahd when he was mortally wounded hundreds of years ago, while he was still alive. In gratitude, he promised them safe passage through his lands. The agreement still holds: the Vistani can come and go through the mists.

Many Barovians distrust the Vistani because they believe them to be Strahd's spies.
