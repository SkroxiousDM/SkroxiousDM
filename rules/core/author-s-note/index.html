Why d12? It’s the only Platonic solid with pentagonal faces. Plato associated the dodecahedron with the cosmos, which I think is fitting for something used to create worlds.

Why A4? Because the A-series paper system possesses a simple elegance: a sheet folded in half across its longer dimension retains the same proportions. North America’s stubborn adherence to its untidy measurement system baffles me. Even Liberia and Myanmar have taken steps towards metrication in the twenty-first century. I suppose we must also wait patiently for North America to relinquish its guns and obnoxiously large trucks.

In the meantime, I’ll settle for dice and paper. They seem a more sensible starting point for building worlds.
