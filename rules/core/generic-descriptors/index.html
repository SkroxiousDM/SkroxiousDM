Twenty-four setting-neutral adjectives open the sentence: “I am a [Descriptor]…” Six belong to each pool. Each one gives +2 to that pool, two double-sided tags and a suggested Instinct. Setting books add Descriptors written for their worlds.

1. Descriptor Rules

Each Descriptor gives (Core Rules §3.1):

Element

Detail

Pool bonus

+2 to one pool

Tags

2 fixed double-sided tags (each can supply Power or be compelled for Tychē Points)

Suggested Instinct

Use it or write your own

Identity

One line linking the two tags; no mechanical effect

Each tag has two sides. Each tag describes something true about your character that can be useful or troublesome. When it helps your action, use it as a power tag under the normal tag rules. When that same trait creates a meaningful complication, it can be compelled under the normal compel rules (Core Rules §3.4, §4). Neither tag is assigned a helpful or troublesome role: both can do either. The strength and complication must follow naturally from the same phrase. The identity line grants no extra modifier or reward.

Example: Built Like a Door can supply Power when you force a door or brace against a blow. It can be compelled when your bulk prevents a discreet approach or passage through a cramped space. Let Me Carry That helps you haul burdens or carry someone to safety, but can be compelled when you take on a load you should have shared or left behind.

Each entry also has a hook: one question to answer at character creation. The answer often becomes your first Belief or a relationship.

Every Descriptor works in any genre. Choose one from this chapter or, when playing in a setting, from that setting book’s Descriptors.

3. Descriptors

1. Burly

You fill doorways. People step aside before you ask.

  • Identity: Your size clears a path, and it also means you’re never easy to miss.

  • Pool: +2 Sōma

  • Suggested Instinct: "If something is heavy, I pick it up myself."

  • Hook: What did you carry, haul or break before this life began?

  • Tags:

Built Like a Door

Power: Lift, break, brace, or physically intimidate.
Compel: Your bulk prevents a discreet approach or passage through a cramped space.

Let Me Carry That

Power: Haul burdens or carry someone to safety.
Compel: You take on a load you should have shared or left behind.

2. Charismatic

When you speak, people stop and listen, whether they want to or not.

  • Identity: You draw attention because you want it, and losing an audience’s favour stings.

  • Pool: +2 Pneuma

  • Suggested Instinct: "If a crowd gathers, I take the stage."

  • Hook: Who once followed you, and where did you lead them?

  • Tags:

All Eyes Turn to Me

Power: Hold attention, command a room, or distract an audience.
Compel: You attract recognition when you need anonymity.

I Give Them Someone to Believe In

Power: Inspire confidence and win support.
Compel: People expect you to live up to the person you presented.

3. Compassionate

You have never walked past someone in pain. You have paid for that more than once.

  • Identity: Other people’s suffering moves you to act. The kindness that opens hearts also makes it hard to walk away.

  • Pool: +2 Pneuma

  • Suggested Instinct: "If someone is hurting, I stop to help."

  • Hook: Whose suffering did you fail to ease, and does it still weigh on you?

  • Tags:

Everyone Deserves Kindness

Power: Comfort someone, earn trust, or defuse hostility.
Compel: You extend kindness to someone who exploits it.

Your Pain Is My Concern

Power: Recognise distress and reach someone who is suffering.
Compel: You interrupt an urgent task to help someone in need.

4. Cunning

You see the board, the pieces and the hand moving them.

  • Identity: You see the hidden angle, and that confidence makes it hard to trust a plan you didn’t make.

  • Pool: +2 Psychē

  • Suggested Instinct: "If someone offers a deal, I look for the catch."

  • Hook: Whose plan did you once see through, and what did it cost them?

  • Tags:

Three Moves Ahead

Power: Anticipate reactions and prepare a clever approach.
Compel: You overcomplicate a straightforward problem or rely on a predicted response.

I Keep Something in Reserve

Power: Conceal leverage, intentions, or a fallback.
Compel: Withholding something important leaves your allies unprepared.

5. Curious

A locked door is a question. You've never been good at leaving questions alone.

  • Identity: The hunger that uncovers answers also pulls you toward things better left closed.

  • Pool: +2 Psychē

  • Suggested Instinct: "If something is hidden, I look inside."

  • Hook: What secret did you uncover that you wish you hadn't?

  • Tags:

I Have to Know Why

Power: Investigate causes and question easy answers.
Compel: You pursue an answer when leaving would be wiser.

Every Secret Is an Invitation

Power: Notice concealment and uncover hidden information.
Compel: You cross a boundary someone deliberately established.

6. Furtive

You keep to walls and corners. Being noticed has never gone well for you.

  • Identity: You’re at home in the margins and exposed without them.

  • Pool: +2 Mētis

  • Suggested Instinct: "If I enter a room, I check the shadows first."

  • Hook: Who are you keeping out of sight of, and why?

  • Tags:

I Keep to the Edges

Power: Avoid notice and observe without drawing attention.
Compel: Staying peripheral keeps you from influencing an important moment.

I Never Show My Whole Hand

Power: Conceal your intentions and protect sensitive information.
Compel: Your evasiveness makes an ally doubt you.

7. Hardy

Cold, hunger and pain are old companions. You've learned to walk on with them.

  • Identity: You endure what others can’t, including hurts you should be tending.

  • Pool: +2 Sōma

  • Suggested Instinct: "If I'm hurt, I keep going until the job is done."

  • Hook: What hardship did you survive that killed others?

  • Tags:

Hardship Is an Old Companion

Power: Endure exposure, deprivation, and punishing conditions.
Compel: You underestimate hardship that your companions cannot withstand.

I Can Keep Going

Power: Continue through fatigue and discomfort.
Compel: You refuse needed rest or treatment.

8. Haunted

Something from your past follows you. Some nights you can almost hear it.

  • Identity: What you survived taught you about the dark, and it still follows you.

  • Pool: +2 Pneuma

  • Suggested Instinct: "If I'm alone at night, I keep a light on."

  • Hook: What did you see that you can never forget?

  • Tags:

I Know What Darkness Looks Like

Power: Recognise familiar horrors and understand others who have suffered.
Compel: A resemblance to the past makes you misjudge the present.

I Carry the Past With Me

Power: Draw resolve or insight from what you survived.
Compel: A memory, reminder, or unfinished obligation demands your attention.

9. Light-Fingered

Things find their way into your hands. You rarely remember deciding to take them.

  • Identity: Your hands are fast, and they itch to prove it.

  • Pool: +2 Mētis

  • Suggested Instinct: "If something valuable is left unattended, I take it."

  • Hook: What is the one thing you stole that you can never give back?

  • Tags:

My Hands Have a Mind of Their Own

Power: Palm, plant, or manipulate something without drawing notice.
Compel: You reach for something before considering who is watching.

Unattended Means Unclaimed

Power: Spot opportunities to acquire something useful.
Compel: You take something whose owner will want it back.

10. Loyal

You chose your people. Nothing will make you abandon them.

  • Identity: Your devotion holds firm, even when the people you trust don’t deserve it.

  • Pool: +2 Pneuma

  • Suggested Instinct: "If someone I trust is threatened, I step between them and it."

  • Hook: Who did you swear to stand by, and have they earned it?

  • Tags:

I Stand by My Own

Power: Protect, support, or speak for your people.
Compel: You take their side when doing so puts you in the wrong.

I Remember Who Stood With Me

Power: Strengthen an old bond or rally someone through shared history.
Compel: An old loyalty conflicts with your present responsibilities.

11. Methodical

Haste makes mistakes. You check everything twice and trust your notes.

  • Identity: Care makes your work reliable and makes you slow when time runs short.

  • Pool: +2 Psychē

  • Suggested Instinct: "If I'm unsure, I make a list."

  • Hook: What disaster taught you never to skip a step?

  • Tags:

Measure Twice, Cut Once

Power: Plan carefully and avoid preventable mistakes.
Compel: You lose an opportunity while checking your work.

Everything in Its Proper Order

Power: Organise a complicated task or follow a demanding procedure.
Compel: Disruption leaves you struggling to improvise.

12. Nimble

You move like water, over walls and through gaps others never see.

  • Identity: Your agility carries you through, until your confidence outruns the ground.

  • Pool: +2 Mētis

  • Suggested Instinct: "If the ground gives way, I move before I think."

  • Hook: What did you learn to escape from, and who taught you?

  • Tags:

I Trust My Feet

Power: Balance, climb, tumble, or cross precarious ground.
Compel: You commit to footing that cannot support you.

There Is Always a Way Through

Power: Slip through gaps and navigate crowded or obstructed spaces.
Compel: You take a route your companions cannot follow.

13. Observant

You notice the scuffed boot, the nervous glance, the window left open.

  • Identity: You notice everything, and sometimes that means you notice the wrong thing.

  • Pool: +2 Psychē

  • Suggested Instinct: "If I enter a place, I count the exits and the people."

  • Hook: What small detail once saved your life?

  • Tags:

The Small Things Matter

Power: Notice subtle clues and changes.
Compel: A minor detail draws you away from the larger danger.

I Keep an Eye on Everyone

Power: Track movements and notice suspicious behaviour.
Compel: Your scrutiny makes someone feel watched or distrusted.

14. Reckless

Thinking is for later. Right now, something needs doing.

  • Identity: Your boldness gets you there first, before anyone has checked what’s waiting.

  • Pool: +2 Sōma

  • Suggested Instinct: "If there's danger ahead, I go at it head-on."

  • Hook: Who got hurt the last time you charged in?

  • Tags:

First Into Trouble

Power: Seize the initiative and act before hesitation takes hold.
Compel: You enter danger before your allies are ready.

I Like My Odds

Power: Commit decisively to a dangerous opportunity.
Compel: You accept a gamble you cannot afford to lose.

15. Relentless

You don't stop. Not when you're tired, not when you're hurt, not when it's hopeless.

  • Identity: You keep going after others stop, even when stopping would be wiser.

  • Pool: +2 Sōma

  • Suggested Instinct: "If I start a chase, I finish it."

  • Hook: What did you chase and never catch?

  • Tags:

I Finish What I Start

Power: Persist through setbacks and sustained effort.
Compel: You refuse to abandon a task that no longer serves its purpose.

I Never Let the Trail Go Cold

Power: Maintain a pursuit and keep pressure on a quarry.
Compel: You follow too far or neglect something more urgent.

16. Resolute

Your word is iron. Once you decide, the world bends or breaks against you.

  • Identity: Your will doesn’t bend, even when the situation demands it.

  • Pool: +2 Pneuma

  • Tags: My Word Is Iron, I Know What I Stand For

  • Suggested Instinct: "If I give my word, I keep it."

  • Hook: What promise are you still keeping, and to whom?

My Word Is Iron

Power: Inspire trust and withstand pressure to break a promise.
Compel: Keeping your word demands a serious sacrifice.

I Know What I Stand For

Power: Resist intimidation, temptation, and manipulation.
Compel: You reject a necessary compromise.

17. Restless

Stillness itches. You need to move, act, see what is over the next hill.

  • Identity: Your energy keeps you moving, and patience is the price.

  • Pool: +2 Mētis

  • Suggested Instinct: "If things go quiet, I go looking for trouble."

  • Hook: What place could never hold you, and who did you leave there?

  • Tags:

I Need to Keep Moving

Power: Sustain motion and respond quickly to a changing situation.
Compel: Waiting in place becomes intolerable.

Something Better Lies Ahead

Power: Find a new route or rally yourself to move beyond a setback.
Compel: You abandon something worthwhile for an uncertain possibility.

18. Scholarly

You have read more than most people will ever hear spoken.

  • Identity: You know a great deal from study and less from hard experience.

  • Pool: +2 Psychē

  • Suggested Instinct: "If a problem is new, I look for precedent."

  • Hook: Which book or teacher shaped you, and what did it leave unanswered?

  • Tags:

I Have Read About This

Power: Recall relevant knowledge and recognise a precedent.
Compel: You trust a written account over contradictory experience.

There Must Be a Source

Power: Research a question and verify a claim.
Compel: You delay action while seeking an authority or reference.

19. Serene

Storms rage around you. Inside, you are still water.

  • Identity: Your calm steadies others, and it can leave you still when action is needed.

  • Pool: +2 Pneuma

  • Suggested Instinct: "If panic spreads, I stay still and speak softly."

  • Hook: What taught you peace, and what would shatter it?

  • Tags:

Still Water in a Storm

Power: Remain composed and steady others during a crisis.
Compel: Your composure is mistaken for indifference when someone needs visible concern.

I Will Hear You Out

Power: Listen patiently and defuse a confrontation.
Compel: You give someone time to stall, escape, or prepare.

20. Sly

There is always another way in, another word to say, another deal to make.

  • Identity: You always find an angle, and people have learned to doubt your word.

  • Pool: +2 Mētis

  • Suggested Instinct: "If there's a shortcut, I find it."

  • Hook: Who did you outwit, and are they still looking for you?

  • Tags:

There Is Always Another Angle

Power: Find a loophole, shortcut, or indirect approach.
Compel: You complicate a situation rather than accept an honest answer.

Why Pay the Asking Price?

Power: Bargain, bluff, or find cheaper leverage.
Compel: You insult someone or jeopardise a fair agreement by pushing for more.

21. Steady

Your hands don't shake. When everyone else panics, you are the one who aims.

  • Identity: Your control is precise because you take your time to find it.

  • Pool: +2 Mētis

  • Suggested Instinct: "If the moment is tense, I breathe and take my time."

  • Hook: When did your steadiness matter most, and who was counting on you?

  • Tags:

I Take the Time to Get It Right

Power: Aim carefully or perform delicate work.
Compel: You hesitate when speed matters more than precision.

I Hold Until the Moment Comes

Power: Maintain a position or wait for the right opening.
Compel: You remain committed after the useful moment has passed.

22. Stubborn

You've been told no all your life. You've ignored it all your life.

  • Identity: You hold your ground, including when the ground is wrong.

  • Pool: +2 Sōma

  • Suggested Instinct: "If someone tells me to stop, I dig in."

  • Hook: What did everyone say was impossible that you did anyway?

  • Tags:

You Will Have to Move Me

Power: Hold your ground against force or pressure.
Compel: You refuse a sensible retreat or change of position.

I Decide When I Am Done

Power: Push through discouragement and others’ expectations.
Compel: You reject good advice because it sounds like an order to quit.

23. Suspicious

Everyone wants something. You just haven't worked out what yet.

  • Identity: Your wariness spots real danger and invents some too.

  • Pool: +2 Psychē

  • Suggested Instinct: "If a stranger is too friendly, I watch their hands."

  • Hook: Who betrayed you, and how did you not see it coming?

  • Tags:

Everyone Wants Something

Power: Identify motives and hidden interests.
Compel: You treat sincere help as an attempted manipulation.

Trust Comes After Proof

Power: Verify claims and take precautions against betrayal.
Compel: You withhold trust when cooperation cannot wait.

24. Weathered

Wind, sun and years have worn you down to something hard and lasting.

  • Identity: Hard years taught you to stay calm, and they left marks that still hurt.

  • Pool: +2 Sōma

  • Suggested Instinct: "If conditions turn harsh, I take the worst of it first."

  • Hook: Where did you earn your scars, and who was with you?

  • Tags:

I Have Survived Worse

Power: Endure familiar hardship and reassure less experienced companions.
Compel: You dismiss a danger because an earlier ordeal seemed worse.

My Scars Taught Me

Power: Recognise hazards and apply hard-earned practical lessons.
Compel: An old injury or painful association complicates a familiar task.
