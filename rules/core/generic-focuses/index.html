1. Focus Rules

1.1 What a Focus Gives You

Element

Detail

Power tags

2

Weakness tag

1 (can be compelled for Tychē Points)

PC link

A prompt that connects you to another player character

Tier 1 ability

Fixed; gained at creation for free

Tier 2 ability

Choose 1 of 2; after 3 Advances, costs an Advance

Tier 3 ability

Choose 1 of 2; after 6 Advances, costs an Advance

  • The other option: once you've reached a tier, you may take its unchosen option later as a normal Advance.

  • ★ Major: costs 1 extra Refresh when taken (Generic Playbooks §1.2). Every Focus has exactly one ★ option, always at Tier 3, so you can always choose a path that keeps your Refresh.

  • Any playbook can take any Focus. The "Suits" line is a suggestion.

  • Changing Focus: at a major milestone, with GM approval, you can swap your Focus. You keep Tier 1 of the new one and rebuy higher tiers with Advances.

1.2 Weird Focuses ◈

Focuses marked ◈ Weird assume the supernatural, impossible technology, or both. In mundane settings the GM removes them from the list. Every other Focus works in any genre.

Module abilities. An ability marked (Module A), (Module B) or (Module C) needs that module. Without it, take the other ability at that tier, or agree a substitute with the GM. Lives Off the Land (marked B in §2) needs Module B throughout; without it, the GM removes it from the list.

1.3 Ability Limits

Focus abilities use the same four fields (Trigger, Cost, Benefit, Limit) and follow the Ability limits in Generic Playbooks §1.2 (free costs, no extra turns, separate bonuses, protected payments). In addition, so the entries don’t repeat them:

  • Free bonus dice still count toward the +2 bonus die maximum.

  • An ability that lets a tag exceed the tag cap adds at most +1 above it (maximum +3), and only for the tag it names.

  • Cost reductions: for Effects other than Create, only one reduction applies to a single Effect. Create is the exception: each stated Create discount lowers the cost by 1 Power, to a minimum of 1 (Core Rules §5).

  • Bonus clock segments advance only the clock they name.

  • Difficulty reductions apply only when a roll tests against a Difficulty, including an independent conflict test; they never modify totals in a versus test (Core Rules §10.5).

  • Crews, packs and allies that act in a conflict are one participant in your side’s script and rotation, with no Disposition track or injury trade of their own. Free Help waives only the pool-point cost.

  • Healing treats existing statuses. It doesn’t restore Disposition, refresh an injury trade, erase a Scar or lift the 1-tier lasting-injury limit (Core Rules §6.6), and a Taken Out ally returns to an ongoing conflict only when an ability says so (Core Rules §6.4).

3. Combat Focuses

1. Never Leaves Anyone Behind

Loyal to a fault. You go back for the fallen, every time.

  • Power tags: I've got your back, carry them out

  • Weakness tag: can't walk away

  • PC link: "I once failed to save someone. [PC] reminds me of them."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Shield Bearer

Trigger: An ally next to you takes an imposed status. Use it before they Resist.
Cost: 1 point from any pool.
Benefit: Reduce the status by 1 tier.
Limit: Before their Resistance. Protected payments can’t be reduced (Generic Playbooks §1.2).

2a

Second Wind

Trigger: You succeed on a Defend and can reach a Taken Out ally.
Cost: 2 Power, instead of recovering Disposition.
Benefit: Bring them back: reduce the status that took them out to tier 4. They rejoin your side’s rotation without an immediate turn.
Limit: Once per scene. Death, a settled defeat, other statuses and Scars stay.

2b

Cover

Trigger: You accept an injury to cancel Disposition loss from an Attack or Feint.
Cost: The accepted injury.
Benefit: Cancel 1 additional Disposition loss, up to the loss inflicted.
Limit: All normal injury-trade restrictions apply.

3a

No One Left

Trigger: Before a volley is revealed, you declare a coordinated withdrawal, and you or an ally succeed on a Defend or Maneuver in that volley.
Cost: You give up the contested objective unless it was escape.
Benefit: After that volley, your side escapes together, with any Taken Out allies you can reach and carry. The opposition gets any compatible part of its intent but can’t capture or separate those you extract.
Limit: Once per session. No other compromise applies; injuries and paid costs stay.

3b

★ Living Wall

Trigger: You take up the position. Until your next action, imposed harm is aimed at allies within reach that you can intercept.
Cost: The harm hits you instead. Pay your own Resistance costs.
Benefit: Redirect it before their Resistance and use your own reduction; Resist each with Edge +1.
Limit: Once per scene, until your next action. Area effects that already hit you both aren’t redirected.

A veteran dragging a wounded comrade out of the line of fire.

2. Leads From the Front

Captain, chief, or sergeant: people follow you.

  • Power tags: born to command, they believe in me

  • Weakness tag: every loss is on me

  • PC link: "[PC] was the first person who ever followed my lead."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Rally

Trigger: You rally allies who can hear you.
Cost: 1 Weal.
Benefit: Restore 2 points to one pool of every ally who can hear you.
Limit: Once per scene.

2a

Battle Plan

Trigger: A conflict starts, after the opposing side commits its first script and before yours.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Look at one of its actions.
Limit: Its first script only.

2b

Lead by Example

Trigger: You succeed with 3+ Power.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: The next ally who acts toward the same goal gets a free bonus die.
Limit: The next ally only. Unused, it ends at the end of the scene. The bonus-dice cap applies (§1.3).

3a

★ Warband

Trigger: Your loyal crew (a group with 2 power tags and 1 weakness) helps or acts.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: It gives free Help, holds a position or acts as one group, and takes statuses as a single target (§1.3).
Limit: Free Help once per scene. It is one participant in your side’s script and rotation (§1.3).

3b

Unbreak-able Line

Trigger: Your side’s Disposition would drop to 0.
Cost: You take a tier-2 Personal status as an accepted payment. You name it (exhausted-2, battered-2) and the GM approves it. It recovers under the normal rules (Core Rules §6.5–6.6).
Benefit: Your side’s Disposition stays at 1.
Limit: Once per conflict.

3. Masters a Weapon

Years of drill with one weapon: a blade, a rifle, a staff, bare fists.

  • Power tags: one with my [weapon], drilled every day

  • Weakness tag: lost without it

  • PC link: "[PC] once saw me lose a duel, and kept it secret."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Signature Style

Trigger: You use a weapon of your chosen type.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: That weapon’s tag may add +1 above the normal +2 cap (maximum +3 from tags), and it gains a second conflict action tag (e.g. a sword also gives +1 Defend).
Limit: Choose the weapon type when you take this ability. Only that weapon’s tag exceeds the cap.

2a

Riposte

Trigger: In a Fight conflict, you win a versus roll with Defend.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: You may spend any of that Power to remove the opposing side’s Disposition instead of restoring your side’s.
Limit: Fight conflicts only.

2b

Disarming Master

Trigger: You use the Disarm maneuver, or Weaken an opponent outside a conflict.
Cost: Disarm costs 2 Power instead of 3.
Benefit: The reduced Disarm price. Outside conflicts, Weaken can strip an opponent’s weapon tag for the scene.
Limit: Other reductions don’t lower Disarm further. A stripped weapon tag returns at scene end.

3a

Perfect Strike

Trigger: You roll with your weapon. Declare it before spending Power or applying consequences.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Turn one kept die into a 12. If that creates doubles, apply them.
Limit: Once per scene.

3b

★ Weapon Saint

Trigger: You use your weapon.
Cost: No attunement Refresh.
Benefit: Your weapon becomes an artifact (Module A) with one extra power tag and an ability you design with the GM. It never depletes.
Limit: Needs Module A. The designed ability needs GM agreement and should be about as strong as one Tier 2 Focus ability.

4. Fights Dirty

Honour is for people who can afford to lose.

  • Power tags: no rules in a fight, eye for a cheap shot

  • Weakness tag: no one trusts me in a fair fight

  • PC link: "I taught [PC] that winning is the only thing that matters. They're still deciding if I was right."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Cheap Shot

Trigger: You make an Attack.
Cost: 1 Power.
Benefit: As part of the Attack, the target also takes a tier-1 Personal status from the dirty trick (blinded-1, winded-1). In a conflict, this is in addition to the Disposition the Attack removes.
Limit: Once per scene. Tier 1 only; the target can Resist it normally.

2a

Improvised Arsenal

Trigger: You use anything in reach as a weapon: chairs, bottles, tools.
Cost: No load (Generic Playbooks §2.1).
Benefit: It gives +1 Attack or +1 Maneuver as an action tag.
Limit: Within the tag cap.

2b

Kick Them While They're Down

Trigger: You Attack a target with a harmful status of tier 2+.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Gain +1 Power on the Attack.
Limit: Only on a Success or Success at a Cost; it can’t turn a failure into a success.

3a

Every Trick in the Book

Trigger: You succeed on a Feint in a Fight conflict.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: The Feint removes 1 additional Disposition.
Limit: Fight conflicts only.

3b

★ Nothing Is Off-Limits

Trigger: You declare a hazard Truth that was already there (Gas Leak, Rotten Floor, Loose Cargo).
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Your next roll that uses it gets a free Extra Feat.
Limit: Once per session. The Extra Feat applies to your next roll that uses it only.

4. Stealth & Precision Focuses

5. Moves Like a Shadow

Silent, patient, and impossible to pin down.

  • Power tags: silent as snowfall, at home in darkness

  • Weakness tag: uneasy in the light

  • PC link: "[PC] is the only one who always knows where I am."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Shadow-Born

Trigger: A Situational Condition involving darkness, noise or crowds helps your Stealth roll.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Treat its rating as 1 higher for this roll.
Limit: Only those Conditions, and only on Stealth. Maximum effective rating 6; only the highest helpful Situational Condition applies. It doesn’t create concealment where none is possible.

2a

Vanish

Trigger: After any roll, if there’s any cover nearby.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Become hidden. No roll needed.
Limit: Once per scene. This changes your position from now on; it doesn’t undo anything already resolved.

2b

Shadow Escort

Trigger: Allies sneak with you.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Up to two allies can use your Stealth result instead of rolling their own.
Limit: Two allies at most. They must be with you, sneaking past the same watchers toward the same goal.

3a

Unseen Hand

Trigger: You succeed on any action while hidden.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Gain +1 Power.
Limit: It doesn’t stack with Opportunist.

3b

★ Living Shadow

Trigger: You describe a concealed route through one guarded or blocked area to a destination beyond it.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: If the route could physically fit you, you reach it unnoticed without a roll; ordinary locks, patrols and surveillance don’t stop you. In a scripted conflict, use it between exchanges: if unnoticed passage was your only objective, you achieve it and leave without compromise; otherwise, continue from the new position.
Limit: Once per session. It moves only you and what you carry.

6. Never Misses

Sniper, archer, gunslinger: steady breath and a sharp eye.

  • Power tags: steady breath, eye like a hawk

  • Weakness tag: clumsy up close

  • PC link: "[PC] owes their life to a shot I made."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Called Shot

Trigger: You roll Shoot.
Cost: No cost for the Weaken.
Benefit: A free Weaken effect: suppress one of the target’s tags (weapon, cover, mobility) for the scene.
Limit: Once per roll, and only on a Success or Success at a Cost.

2a

Over-watch

Trigger: An imposed status of tier 2 or less is about to hit an ally; or, in a conflict, covering fire can protect your side.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Cancel the status by shooting its source; declare it before the status applies. In conflicts, you may Defend with Shoot when covering fire can protect your side.
Limit: The cancel is once per scene.

2b

Trick Shot

Trigger: You shoot past cover or angles, or shoot an object (cut a rope, hit a switch, ricochet).
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Ignore harmful Situational Conditions from cover and awkward angles. Gain a free Extra Feat when you shoot objects.
Limit: Normal Extra Feat permissions apply.

3a

One Shot

Trigger: You succeed on a Shoot Attack against a non-major NPC.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: The Attack Takes them Out. In a conflict, it removes that participant and 1 additional Disposition.
Limit: Once per session. Non-major NPCs only.

3b

★ Sniper's Patience

Trigger: You spend a scene setting up a shot (4-segment clock).
Cost: The setup scene. Fill the clock with preparation rolls (Notice, Stealth, Shoot) at 1 Power per segment (Advance). On a failed preparation roll, the GM’s move can advance a danger: the target moves, a guard notices.
Benefit: Once the clock is full, your shot gets +2 bonus dice for free and +2 Power on a success.
Limit: That shot only.

Art placeholder
An archer drawing breath before a long shot across a valley.

7. Opens Every Lock

Locks, safes, firewalls, sealed doors: they're puzzles, and you love puzzles.

  • Power tags: nothing stays closed, thinks like an architect

  • Weakness tag: can't resist a challenge

  • PC link: "[PC] keeps me out of trouble, or tries to."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Bypass

Trigger: You roll against a lock, safe or security clock.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Before you roll, you know the clock’s size and what failing will set off. Each success advances it 1 extra segment.
Limit: Bonus segments advance that clock only (§1.3).

2a

Back Door

Trigger: You use Create to establish access or leverage at your current location.
Cost: 1 Power.
Benefit: Create the Truth I know a way in, naming the route or leverage.
Limit: Once per location. It gives access or leverage, not victory over an unrelated objective. Normal scope and duration apply.

2b

Trap Sense

Trigger: You would trigger a trap or alarm, you Resist a trap, or you warn allies of one in time.
Cost: The normal Resistance cost, reduced as below.
Benefit: You always notice traps and alarms before triggering them. Resisting traps costs 0 on a Resistance result of 5+. Allies you warn in time Resist that trap with Edge +1.
Limit: Allies gain the Edge bonus only against the trap you warned them about.

3a

Master Key

Trigger: You face a lock or security.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Ordinary locks open without a roll. Complex security is one Difficulty step lower.
Limit: An ordinary lock is a single mechanical barrier. Security with alarms, guards, magic or technology layered on it, or with its own clock, is complex. Difficulty reductions apply only against a Difficulty (§1.3).

3b

★ Vault Breaker

Trigger: You declare a security layer already cracked.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Fill one security clock completely.
Limit: Once per mission.

Art placeholder
Steady hands working a lockpick into an ornate safe.

8. Always Has an Escape Plan

You never walk into a room without knowing how to leave it.

  • Power tags: knows every exit, slippery

  • Weakness tag: runs first, thinks later

  • PC link: "[PC] was with me during my worst getaway."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Exit Strategy

Trigger: You enter a new scene.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Ask one free Discover question about exits or escape routes.
Limit: One question per new scene.

2a

Clean Getaway

Trigger: A Chase conflict begins in which you’re fleeing.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Your side gets +2 starting Disposition.
Limit: A flat starting-Disposition bonus (§1.3).

2b

Contin-gency

Trigger: Things go wrong and you reveal a prepared fallback.
Cost: No Weal needed.
Benefit: Establish a justified fallback (stashed gear, a getaway vehicle, a safe house) as a Truth, at whatever scale fits (Core Rules §8.2). It lasts until the fallback is spent: the vehicle drives off, the gear is taken, the safe house is used or found.
Limit: Once per mission. It can’t contradict resolved events, and it gives no Situational Condition rating or automatic escape.

3a

Not Here

Trigger: You would receive an imposed harmful status and can act.
Cost: 1 Weal.
Benefit: Describe how you evade its source: cancel that status on yourself and move to a nearby position.
Limit: Once per scene. Disposition loss and other effects of the triggering action still apply.

3b

★ Dead Man's Switch

Trigger: You’re captured or confined, and you declare a contingency.
Cost: Give the GM 2 Woe.
Benefit: At the start of your next scene it creates an opening (you slip one restraint, an exit opens, or an established contact helps), and you may at once make one escape roll, even if confinement would normally prevent it. In a conflict, resolve it before the next exchange.
Limit: Once per session. Your statuses stay, and it doesn’t revive you, recover confiscated gear, free companions or guarantee the roll.

A thief slipping out a high window as the door bursts open.

5. Knowledge Focuses

9. Builds Impossible Things

An artificer, engineer, or inventor who makes what shouldn't work, work.

  • Power tags: workshop hands, it'll work, trust me

  • Weakness tag: never quite finished

  • PC link: "I built [PC]'s gear, and I'm still tinkering with it."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Jury-Rig

Trigger: You Craft a temporary gadget.
Cost: 1 Power.
Benefit: Create a Truth that lasts the scene, or instead a helpful rating-1 Situational Condition from the gadget (makeshift brace 1).
Limit: Once per scene. One Truth or one Condition, rating 1 only.

2a

Inventor

Trigger: During downtime, you work on a gadget.
Cost: A 6-segment clock.
Benefit: Turn the gadget into a permanent item with 1 power tag and 1 action tag.
Limit: Count its benefit once per roll.

2b

Field Repairs

Trigger: You Restore gear, vehicles or constructs, or repair damaged armour.
Cost: The normal Restore cost.
Benefit: Restore effects work on gear, vehicles and constructs. Repair damaged armour without downtime.
Limit: No additional limit.

3a

Master-work

Trigger: During downtime, you work on a masterwork.
Cost: A 12-segment downtime clock.
Benefit: Create one artifact (Module A). You pay no Refresh to attune your own masterwork.
Limit: Needs Module A. One masterwork at a time: you can start another only after the current one is lost, destroyed or given away.

3b

★ Rig the Field

Trigger: You rig the area with gadgets.
Cost: 2 Power total.
Benefit: Create two Truths. You may also give one of them a helpful rating-2 Situational Condition from the rigging, at no extra cost.
Limit: Once per scene. One Condition only, rating 2 at most.

A workbench crowded with a half-built contraption and scattered tools.

10. Heals the Broken

Doctor, field medic, herbalist, or combat surgeon.

  • Power tags: calm hands, bedside manner

  • Weakness tag: can't refuse anyone in pain

  • PC link: "I patched up [PC] when no one else would."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Triage

Trigger: You treat an ally.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Reduce the ally’s harmful status by 1 tier without a roll.
Limit: Once per scene.

2a

Battlefield Surgeon

Trigger: In a conflict, you Defend when you can reach and treat an ally.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: You may Defend with Medicine. On a successful Defend, restore Disposition normally and also clear one tier-1 harmful status from that ally that your treatment can address.
Limit: Only an ally you can reach and treat.

2b

Remedy

Trigger: A Players” Turn (Module B).
Cost: No Check.
Benefit: Clear one Grind hardship from one ally.
Limit: Once per Players” Turn.

3a

Miracle Worker

Trigger: An ally has a tier-6 physical status.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: They return at tier 4, are no longer Taken Out, and may rejoin an unresolved conflict if circumstances allow. A Dying ally stops Dying; this counts as definitive care (Core Rules §6.7). Erase the Scar they gained from reaching tier 6 with this status.
Limit: Once per session. It can’t undo death. A settled defeat stays settled.

3b

★ Physician's Care

Trigger: You treat allies during a Third rest.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: They recover all pool points instead of that rest’s normal recovery.
Limit: The Third rest only.

Art placeholder
A field medic stitching a wound by lamplight.

11. Solves the Unsolvable

Detective, investigator, or analyst: you follow the thread to the end.

  • Power tags: nothing gets past me, follows the thread

  • Weakness tag: obsessed with the case

  • PC link: "[PC] was part of the case that made me."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Case File

Trigger: You start a case, or buy Discover on it.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Start a Mystery clock (6 or 8). Every Discover you buy also advances it 1 segment. When it fills, the GM reveals the core truth.
Limit: One clock per case.

2a

Interro-gator

Trigger: You buy Discover while questioning someone.
Cost: The normal Discover cost.
Benefit: Each Discover gives two answers. You also learn whether the subject believes each answer.
Limit: No additional limit.

2b

Recon-struction

Trigger: You’re at the scene of a past event.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Ask three Discover questions for free.
Limit: Once per location.

3a

The Reveal

Trigger: You publicly name the culprit in a case you’ve investigated and explain the evidence.
Cost: A wrong accusation gives nothing and uses the session’s use.
Benefit: The GM confirms whether you’re right. If you are, your side gains +3 starting Disposition in the next conflict this session in which it confronts the culprit over the case; declare it before Disposition is rolled. If your side wins, the culprit gets no compromise protecting them from the case-related outcome in the stakes; others on their side keep theirs.
Limit: Once per session. It doesn’t prove guilt without evidence, expand the stakes, force a confession or skip a finishing procedure.

3b

★ One Step Ahead

Trigger: The GM spends Woe, before its effect resolves.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Reveal that you anticipated the move: cancel that Woe spend. The Woe returns to the GM.
Limit: Once per session, one spend. The GM can’t spend Woe on the same move again this scene.

Art placeholder
A detective holding a single clue up to the light.

Effect

Echoes of the Past

Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: It is one Difficulty step lower.
Limit: Difficulty reductions apply only against a Difficulty (§1.3).

3b

★ Rewrite History

Trigger: You set out to prove a Campaign Truth false.
Cost: A 12-segment research clock.
Benefit: When the clock fills, the Truth changes (Module C).
Limit: Needs Module C. One Campaign Truth per clock.

Charm, quick wit, and the right words at the right time.

  • PC link: "[PC] has bailed me out more than once; I owe them."

Tier

Ability

Effect

Fast Talk

Trigger: You try to get out of a bad situation.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: Use Persuade or Deceive in place of any other skill.
Limit: Once per scene. This swaps the skill only.

2a

Change of Plans

Trigger: A social roll is a Success at a Cost.
Cost: No additional cost.
Benefit: You succeed without the cost.
Limit: Once per scene.
