1. Focus Rules

1.1 What a Focus Gives You

Element

Detail

Power tags

2

Weakness tag

1 (can be compelled for Tychē Points)

PC link

A prompt that connects you to another player character

Tier 1 ability

Fixed; gained at creation for free

Tier 2 ability

Choose 1 of 2; after 3 Advances, costs an Advance

Tier 3 ability

Choose 1 of 2; after 6 Advances, costs an Advance

  • The other option: once you've reached a tier, you may take its unchosen option later as a normal Advance.

  • ★ Major: costs 1 Refresh when taken (Core Rules §3.6). Every Focus has exactly one ★ option, always at Tier 3, so you can always choose a path that keeps your Refresh.

  • Any playbook can take any Focus. The "Suits" line is a suggestion.

  • Changing Focus: at a major milestone, with GM approval, you can swap your Focus. You keep Tier 1 of the new one and rebuy higher tiers with Advances.

1.2 Weird Focuses ◈

Focuses marked ◈ Weird assume the supernatural, impossible technology, or both. In mundane settings the GM removes them from the list. Every other Focus works in any genre.

Module abilities. An ability marked (Module A), (Module B) or (Module C) needs that module. Without it, take the other ability at that tier, or agree a substitute with the GM. Lives Off the Land (marked B in §2) needs Module B throughout; without it, the GM removes it from the list.

1.3 Ability Limits

Focus abilities follow the Ability limits in Generic Playbooks §1.2 (free costs, no extra turns, separate bonuses, protected payments). In addition, so the entries don’t repeat them:

  • Free bonus dice still count toward the +2 bonus die maximum.

  • An ability that lets a tag exceed the tag cap adds at most +1 above it (maximum +3), and only for the tag it names.

  • Cost reductions: for Effects other than Create, only one reduction applies to a single Effect. Create is the exception: each stated Create discount lowers the cost by 1 Power, to a minimum of 1 (Core Rules §5).

  • Flat starting-Disposition bonuses apply at full value with other flat modifiers, before the minimum of 1 and opposing Superiority (Core Rules §10.2–10.3). Changing conflict type within the same struggle doesn’t refresh Disposition or reapply them.

  • Declared facts (Truths, contingencies, contacts, hazards) can’t contradict resolved events or recalculate Superiority after setup. Bonus clock segments advance that clock only.

  • Difficulty reductions apply only when a roll tests against a Difficulty, including an independent conflict test; they never modify totals in a versus test (Core Rules §10.5).

  • Crews, packs and allies that act in a conflict are one participant in your side’s script and rotation, with no Disposition track or injury trade of their own. Free Help waives only the pool-point cost.

  • Healing treats existing statuses. It doesn’t restore Disposition, refresh an injury trade, erase a Scar or lift the 1-tier lasting-injury limit (Core Rules §6.6), and a Taken Out ally returns to an ongoing conflict only when an ability says so (Core Rules §6.4).

3. Combat Focuses

1. Never Leaves Anyone Behind

Loyal to a fault. You go back for the fallen, every time.

  • Power tags: I've got your back, carry them out

  • Weakness tag: can't walk away

  • PC link: "I once failed to save someone. [PC] reminds me of them."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Shield Bearer

When an ally next to you takes an imposed status, spend 1 point from any pool to reduce it by 1 tier before they Resist.

2a

Second Wind

Once per scene, when you succeed on a Defend and can reach a Taken Out ally, spend 2 Power (instead of recovering Disposition) to bring them back: reduce the status that took them out to tier 4. They rejoin your side's rotation without an immediate turn. Death, a settled defeat, other statuses and Scars stay.

2b

Cover

When you accept an injury to cancel Disposition loss from an Attack or Feint, cancel 1 additional Disposition loss, up to the loss inflicted. All normal injury-trade restrictions apply.

3a

No One Left

Once per session, before a volley is revealed, declare a coordinated withdrawal. After that volley, if you or an ally succeeded on a Defend or Maneuver in it, your side escapes together, with any Taken Out allies you can reach and carry. You give up the contested objective unless it was escape; the opposition gets any compatible part of its intent but can't capture or separate those you extract. No other compromise applies; injuries and paid costs stay.

3b

★ Living Wall

Once per scene, until your next action, imposed harm aimed at allies within reach that you can intercept hits you instead. Redirect it before their Resistance and use your own reduction; Resist each with Edge +1. Area effects that already hit you both aren't redirected.

A veteran dragging a wounded comrade out of the line of fire.

2. Leads From the Front

Captain, chief, or sergeant: people follow you.

  • Power tags: born to command, they believe in me

  • Weakness tag: every loss is on me

  • PC link: "[PC] was the first person who ever followed my lead."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Rally

Once per scene, spend 1 Momentum to restore 2 points to one pool of every ally who can hear you.

2a

Battle Plan

At the start of a conflict, after the opposing side commits its first script and before yours, look at one of its actions.

2b

Lead by Example

When you succeed with 3+ Power, the next ally who acts toward the same goal gets a free bonus die.

3a

★ Warband

Gain a loyal crew (a group with 2 power tags and 1 weakness). It gives free Help once per scene, holds a position or acts as one group, and takes statuses as a single target (§1.3).

3b

Unbreak-able Line

Once per conflict, when your side's Disposition would drop to 0, it stays at 1 and you take a tier-2 status as an accepted payment.

A captain at the front of a charge, banner raised.

3. Masters a Weapon

Years of drill with one weapon: a blade, a rifle, a staff, bare fists.

  • Power tags: one with my [weapon], drilled every day

  • Weakness tag: lost without it

  • PC link: "[PC] once saw me lose a duel, and kept it secret."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Signature Style

Choose a weapon type. That weapon's tag may add +1 above the normal +2 cap (maximum +3 from tags), and it gains a second conflict action tag (e.g. a sword also gives +1 Defend).

2a

Riposte

In Fight conflicts, when you win a versus roll with Defend, you may spend any of that Power to remove the opposing side's Disposition instead of restoring your side's.

2b

Disarming Master

The Disarm maneuver costs 2 Power instead of 3; other reductions don't lower it further. Outside conflicts, Weaken can strip an opponent's weapon tag for the scene.

3a

Perfect Strike

Once per scene, after rolling with your weapon, turn one kept die into a 12. If that creates doubles, apply them. Declare it before spending Power or applying consequences.

3b

★ Weapon Saint

Your weapon becomes an artifact (Module A) with one extra power tag and an ability you design with the GM. It never depletes and costs no attunement Refresh.

Art placeholder
A fighter mid-drill with one well-worn weapon.

4. Fights Dirty

Honour is for people who can afford to lose.

  • Power tags: no rules in a fight, eye for a cheap shot

  • Weakness tag: no one trusts me in a fair fight

  • PC link: "I taught [PC] that winning is the only thing that matters. They're still deciding if I was right."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Cheap Shot

Once per scene, as part of an Attack, Create a Truth (sand in the eyes, low blow) for 1 Power. This permits only the Create purchase on that Attack.

2a

Improvised Arsenal

Anything in reach is a weapon for you: chairs, bottles, tools. It gives +1 Attack or +1 Maneuver as an action tag (within the tag cap) and costs no load (Generic Playbooks §2.1).

2b

Kick Them While They're Down

Against a target with a harmful status of tier 2+, gain +1 Power on Attack.

3a

Every Trick in the Book

In Fight conflicts, your successful Feints remove 1 additional Disposition.

3b

★ Nothing Is Off-Limits

Once per session, declare a hazard Truth that was already there (gas leak, rotten floor, loose cargo). Your next roll that uses it gets a free Extra Feat.

4. Stealth & Precision Focuses

5. Moves Like a Shadow

Silent, patient, and impossible to pin down.

  • Power tags: silent as snowfall, at home in darkness

  • Weakness tag: uneasy in the light

  • PC link: "[PC] is the only one who always knows where I am."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Shadow-Born

Truths about darkness, noise, or crowds lower your Stealth Difficulty by two steps instead of one.

2a

Vanish

Once per scene, after any roll, become hidden if there's any cover nearby. No roll needed. This changes your position from now on; it doesn't undo anything already resolved.

2b

Shadow Escort

Up to two allies can use your Stealth result instead of rolling their own.

3a

Unseen Hand

Any action you take while hidden gains +1 Power on a success. It doesn't stack with Opportunist.

3b

★ Living Shadow

Once per session, describe a concealed route through one guarded or blocked area to a destination beyond it. If the route could physically fit you, you reach it unnoticed without a roll; ordinary locks, patrols and surveillance don't stop you. It moves only you and what you carry. In a scripted conflict, use it between exchanges: if unnoticed passage was your only objective, you achieve it and leave without compromise; otherwise, continue from the new position.

Art placeholder
A cloaked figure flattened against a wall as a lantern passes.

6. Never Misses

Sniper, archer, gunslinger: steady breath and a sharp eye.

  • Power tags: steady breath, eye like a hawk

  • Weakness tag: clumsy up close

  • PC link: "[PC] owes their life to a shot I made."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Called Shot

Once per roll with Shoot, a free Weaken effect: suppress one of the target's tags (weapon, cover, mobility) for the scene.

2a

Over-watch

Once per scene, cancel an imposed status of tier 2 or less about to hit an ally by shooting the source; declare it before the status applies. In conflicts, you may Defend with Shoot when covering fire can protect your side.

2b

Trick Shot

Ignore Truths about cover and angles. Gain a free Extra Feat when you shoot objects (cut a rope, hit a switch, ricochet).

3a

One Shot

Once per session, a successful Shoot Attack against a non-major NPC Takes them Out. In a conflict, it removes that participant and 1 additional Disposition.

3b

★ Sniper's Patience

Spend a scene setting up (4-segment clock). Your shot gets +2 bonus dice for free and +2 Power on a success.

Art placeholder
An archer drawing breath before a long shot across a valley.

7. Opens Every Lock

Locks, safes, firewalls, sealed doors: they're puzzles, and you love puzzles.

  • Power tags: nothing stays closed, thinks like an architect

  • Weakness tag: can't resist a challenge

  • PC link: "[PC] keeps me out of trouble, or tries to."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Bypass

Each success on a lock, safe or security clock advances it 1 extra segment. Before you roll, you also know the clock's size and what failing will set off.

2a

Back Door

Once per location, Create the Truth I know a way in for 1 Power. It gives access or leverage, not victory over an unrelated objective.

2b

Trap Sense

You always notice traps and alarms before triggering them. Resisting traps costs 0 on a Resistance result of 5+. Allies you warn in time Resist that trap with Edge +1.

3a

Master Key

Ordinary locks open without a roll. Complex security is one Difficulty step lower.

3b

★ Vault Breaker

Once per mission, declare a security layer already cracked: fill one security clock completely.

Art placeholder
Steady hands working a lockpick into an ornate safe.

8. Always Has an Escape Plan

You never walk into a room without knowing how to leave it.

  • Power tags: knows every exit, slippery

  • Weakness tag: runs first, thinks later

  • PC link: "[PC] was with me during my worst getaway."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Exit Strategy

When you enter a new scene, ask one free Discover question about exits or escape routes.

2a

Clean Getaway

In Chase conflicts where you're fleeing, your side gets +2 starting Disposition.

2b

Contin-gency

Once per mission, when things go wrong, reveal a prepared fallback (stashed gear, a getaway vehicle, a safe house) as a Truth. No Momentum needed.

3a

Not Here

Once per scene, when you would receive an imposed harmful status and can act, spend 1 Momentum and describe how you evade its source: cancel that status on yourself and move to a nearby position. Disposition loss and other effects of the triggering action still apply.

3b

★ Dead Man's Switch

Once per session, when you're captured or confined, declare a contingency and give the GM 2 Woe. At the start of your next scene it creates an opening (you slip one restraint, an exit opens, or an established contact helps), and you may at once make one escape roll, even if confinement would normally prevent it. In a conflict, resolve it before the next exchange. Your statuses stay, and it doesn't revive you, recover confiscated gear, free companions or guarantee the roll.

A thief slipping out a high window as the door bursts open.

5. Knowledge Focuses

9. Builds Impossible Things

An artificer, engineer, or inventor who makes what shouldn't work, work.

  • Power tags: workshop hands, it'll work, trust me

  • Weakness tag: never quite finished

  • PC link: "I built [PC]'s gear, and I'm still tinkering with it."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Jury-Rig

Once per scene, Craft a temporary gadget: Create a Truth for 1 Power that lasts the scene.

2a

Inventor

During downtime, turn a gadget into a permanent item with a 6-segment clock. It gets 1 power tag and 1 action tag; count its benefit once per roll.

2b

Field Repairs

Restore effects work on gear, vehicles, and constructs. Repair damaged armour without downtime.

3a

Master-work

Create one artifact (Module A) with a 12-segment downtime clock. You pay no Refresh to attune your own masterwork.

3b

★ Rig the Field

Once per scene, rig the area with gadgets: Create two Truths for 2 Power total.

Art placeholder
A workbench crowded with a half-built contraption and scattered tools.

10. Heals the Broken

Doctor, field medic, herbalist, or combat surgeon.

  • Power tags: calm hands, bedside manner

  • Weakness tag: can't refuse anyone in pain

  • PC link: "I patched up [PC] when no one else would."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Triage

Once per scene, reduce an ally's harmful status by 1 tier without a roll.

2a

Battlefield Surgeon

In conflicts, you may Defend with Medicine when you can reach and treat an ally. On a successful Defend, restore Disposition normally and also clear one tier-1 harmful status from that ally that your treatment can address.

2b

Remedy

In each Players' Turn, clear one Grind condition (Module B) from one ally without spending a Check.

3a

Miracle Worker

Once per session, bring an ally back from a tier-6 physical status: they return at tier 4, are no longer Taken Out, and may rejoin an unresolved conflict if circumstances allow. A settled defeat stays settled.

3b

★ Physician's Care

Allies you treat during a Third rest recover all pool points instead of that rest's normal recovery.

Art placeholder
A field medic stitching a wound by lamplight.

11. Solves the Unsolvable

Detective, investigator, or analyst: you follow the thread to the end.

  • Power tags: nothing gets past me, follows the thread

  • Weakness tag: obsessed with the case

  • PC link: "[PC] was part of the case that made me."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Case File

Start a Mystery clock (6 or 8) for a case. Every Discover you buy also advances it 1 segment. When it fills, the GM reveals the core truth.

2a

Interro-gator

When questioning someone, each Discover gives two answers. You also learn whether the subject believes each answer.

2b

Recon-struction

At the scene of a past event, ask three Discover questions for free (once per location).

3a

The Reveal

Once per session, publicly name the culprit in a case you've investigated and explain the evidence. The GM confirms whether you're right. If you are, your side gains +3 starting Disposition in the next conflict this session in which it confronts the culprit over the case; declare it before Disposition is rolled. If your side wins, the culprit gets no compromise protecting them from the case-related outcome in the stakes; others on their side keep theirs. It doesn't prove guilt without evidence, expand the stakes, force a confession or skip a finishing procedure. A wrong accusation gives nothing and uses the session's use.

3b

★ One Step Ahead

Once per session, reveal that you anticipated an opponent's move: cancel one GM Woe spend as it happens, before its effect resolves.

Art placeholder
A detective holding a single clue up to the light.

12. Remembers What Was Forgotten

Historian, archaeologist, or keeper of lost lore.

  • Power tags: walks among ruins, reads dead languages

  • Weakness tag: lives in the past

  • PC link: "[PC] helped me recover something that was lost."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Echoes of the Past

At any old or abandoned place, ask one free Discover question about its history.

2a

Lost Knowledge

Once per session, you've read about this before: Create a Truth (ancient weakness, forgotten route) for 1 Power.

2b

Relic Hunter

When you find cyphers or treasure, roll or draw twice and choose. You identify artifacts automatically.

3a

Living Archive

Lore rolls about history, legends, and old cultures are one Difficulty step lower.

3b

★ Rewrite History

Prove a Campaign Truth is false with a 12-segment research clock. When it fills, the Truth changes (Module C).

6. Social Focuses

13. Talks Their Way Out of Anything

Charm, quick wit, and the right words at the right time.

  • Power tags: always has a story ready, disarming smile

  • Weakness tag: my mouth writes cheques

  • PC link: "[PC] has bailed me out more than once; I owe them."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Fast Talk

Once per scene, use Persuade or Deceive in place of any other skill to get out of a bad situation. This swaps the skill only.

2a

Change of Plans

Once per scene, when a social roll is a Success at a Cost, you succeed without the cost.

2b

Disarming

Once per scene, stop a fight before it starts with a Persuade roll. On a success, the Fight becomes a Negotiation conflict; set the new skills before the next exchange.

3a

★ The Long Con

Start a long con clock (8) against a target. Each success against them Advances it. When it fills, they give up something substantial: land, title, a secret, or their master.

3b

Golden Tongue

Tychē Point invokes on Persuade and Deceive give +3 instead of +2.

Art placeholder
A smooth talker grinning past a pair of suspicious guards.

14. Wears a Hundred Masks

Spy, impostor, or actor: you can be anyone.

  • Power tags: perfect mimic, no one remembers my real face

  • Weakness tag: who am I really?

  • PC link: "[PC] is the only one who knows my real name."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Quick Change

Create a disguise Truth for 1 Power, even without a disguise kit. It doesn't by itself settle a disputed objective.

2a

Mimic

You can copy voices, mannerisms, and handwriting. Impersonating a specific person gets a free bonus die.

2b

Deep Cover

Establish one persistent cover identity with a name, a credible background and one named contact who knows you by it. Record it as a Truth that lasts until the cover is blown. To expose it as false, the GM must name a credible cause and spend 2 Woe; without that, suspicion can create scrutiny or obstacles but doesn't prove the cover false. It doesn't protect unrelated lies, prevent arrest under the cover name or erase earlier evidence, and you can always reveal yourself. A new cover takes downtime.

3a

Belongs Anywhere

Ignore Truths about social barriers (members only, high security, closed community).

3b

★ Become Them

Once per session, after observing a specific NPC and preparing, take their place for one scene. People and ordinary identity checks accept you as them without a roll. This guarantees recognition, not obedience, and gives you none of their memories, abilities or possessions. It ends at scene end, when you reveal yourself, or when someone sees direct proof you can't be them (such as the real NPC beside you); suspicion or an unrelated failed roll isn't enough.

Art placeholder
A row of masks on a dressing table, one missing.

15. Knows Everyone Worth Knowing

Fixer, socialite, or broker: you're one call away from anyone.

  • Power tags: favours owed, always on the guest list

  • Weakness tag: owes favours too

  • PC link: "I introduced [PC] to the people who matter."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Contacts

Start with 3 named contacts (Truths that last until the contact is lost). Once per session, call one for a free Discover or a minor favour.

2a

Favour Bank

In each Players' Turn (Module B), help a contact (spend a Check) to bank a favour. Spend a favour for substantial help: a safe house, troops, a pardon.

2b

Gossip Network

At the start of each session, ask 2 Discover questions about current rumours. When confirming rumours (Module C), roll twice and choose.

3a

Power Broker

Once per World Turn (Module C or E), advance or reverse one faction or project clock by 1.

3b

★ The Right Person

Once per session, exactly the right contact for the situation happens to be present.

Art placeholder
A broker at a crowded party, whispering into an ear.

16. Carries the Weight of a Crown

Noble, officer, executive, or chieftain: you hold authority, and it holds you.

  • Power tags: voice of authority, my name carries weight

  • Weakness tag: duty before self

  • PC link: "[PC] serves me, or is the one person who refuses to."

Tier

Ability

Effect

1

Rank

Choose where your authority applies (a realm, company, fleet, or clan). There, Create the Truth by my order for 1 Power. It gives authority or leverage, not the disputed objective.

2a

Retainers

Gain loyal servants or aides as a group tag. They give free Help once per scene and handle errands, but take no turns in conflicts.

2b

Immunity

Once per session, when an authority that recognises your position would arrest you or impose a legal penalty, invoke your status first. The authority must suspend it and refer the matter to a named superior, court or institution. You stay free pending the hearing, but agree a concrete obligation now: appear at a stated time, provide a guarantor or an equivalent. It protects only you, doesn't erase the charge or undo penalties already applied, and doesn't bind those who reject your authority. In a conflict, it replaces your arrest or legal penalty but not the result, and it can't cancel a penalty you accepted as payment or concession.

3a

Command the Field

In Battle conflicts, your side gets +3 starting Disposition.

3b

★ Holdings

Gain a holding (keep, office, ship, estate) as a site (Module C) with 3 Truths you choose. Once per Players' Turn (Module B), one action there costs no Check.

A ruler on a worn throne, petitioners waiting below.

Handler

Pack Tactics

Discoverer

  • PC link: "I taught [PC] how to survive their first winter."

Ability

Effect

Forager's Eye

Natural Remedies

3a

Hardened

3b

★ Living Larder

20. Was Born Behind the Wheel

Driver, pilot, sailor, or rider: you and your vehicle are one.

Ability

Effect

1

Monster Lore

Once per scene, Discover a supernatural creature's weakness tag without rolling. You also know one thing that repels or binds it (salt, iron, a name).

2a

Bane

Against a creature whose weakness you've exploited, a successful conflict Attack removes 1 additional Disposition, or, if it inflicts a harmful status, raises that status by 1 tier (never both). The first time you exploit a weakness against a creature, also Discover one more of its weaknesses or abilities for free. It adds no status to an ordinary Attack and doesn't improve staking, killing strikes or other finishing procedures.

2b

Ward the Threshold

Spend an uninterrupted scene preparing one clearly defined entrance and state which side it protects. Until the next dawn, supernatural creatures can't cross without overcoming the ward: a Will test against Formidable 21 (Success at a Cost counts). Success lets that creature cross and ends the ward against it; failure leaves it outside. In a scripted conflict, crossing takes a Maneuver resolved as an independent Will test, at most once per exchange per creature, with no ordinary Maneuver effects; the opposing action also resolves independently. The ward doesn't stop attacks or Effects that reach across. Destroying the markings ends it.

Unshake-able

3b

Name a supernatural foe. You always sense its direction, and your rolls against it get a free bonus die.

Design Notes
Coverage. Strong fits by Focus number. Bellator: 1–4, 6, 16, 17, 19, 21. Furcifer: 1, 3–8, 11, 13, 14, 20. Savant: 7, 9–12, 15, 18, 21, 22. Interlocutor: 2, 8, 10, 11, 13–16. Explorator: 1–3, 5, 6, 9, 12, 17–20. Adept: 7, 9, 10, 12, 21–23. Any: 23, 24. Off-type combinations are encouraged: an Interlocutor who Masters a Weapon is a duellist-diplomat; an Adept who Was Born Behind the Wheel is a witch with a haunted truck.

Watch in playtest. The tag-cap exceptions (Masters a Weapon, Is More Than Human) at Formidable and Heroic Difficulty. Focus abilities that echo other abilities (Bypass and Decipher, Trap Sense and Escape Artist, Interrogator and Tracker, Bane and Brutal, Monster Lore and Beast Lore): the shared benefit doesn’t stack on one character, but the Focus’s rider still applies. Stated Create discounts still stack to the minimum (Core Rules §5).

Gaps. Planned Focuses: faith (Keeps the Faith), scholar of war, merchant, performer.
